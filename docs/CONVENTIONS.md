# Conventions, Proof Routes & Verification

Notes on definitions, normalizations, proof routes, scope limits, textbook divergences, and verification in `lean-convex-geometry`.

## Mathematical Conventions

- **Convex bodies (`ConvexBody E`):** Uses Mathlib's `ConvexBody E` (nonempty compact convex sets), which includes degenerate sets with empty interior such as singletons, segments, and radius-zero balls. Theorems needing nonempty interior or `0 ∈ interior K` take that hypothesis explicitly.
- **Volume and spherical measure:** `volume` uses Mathlib's Lebesgue normalization. `sphereProb μ` is the normalized cone probability measure on `Metric.sphere 0 1`. It is independent of the choice or scaling of an additive Haar measure `μ` (`sphereProb_eq_of_isAddHaarMeasure`) and is zero on the empty unit sphere in dimension `0`. Classically this coincides with the unique `O(E)`-invariant probability measure and normalized `(d - 1)`-dimensional Hausdorff surface measure on the sphere, though neither uniqueness nor the Hausdorff-measure identification is formalized here.
- **Width and mean width:** `width K u = supportFn K u + supportFn K (-u)` is the breadth in direction `u`, while `meanWidth K = ∫ u, supportFn K u ∂(sphereProb volume)` is half the classical mean breadth. Thus `meanWidth (bodyClosedBall c r) = r` and `∫ u, width K u ∂(sphereProb volume) = 2 * meanWidth K`.
- **Outer normal cones:** `normalCone S y = {u | ∀ z ∈ S, ⟪z - y, u⟫ ≤ 0}` does not guard on `y ∈ S` (so `0 ∈ normalCone S y` even when `y ∉ S`). `strictNormalCone S y` equals `normalCone S y` for `y ∈ S` and `∅` for `y ∉ S`.
- **Mahler volume:** `mahlerVolume μ S = μ S * μ (polarBody S)` is centered at the origin and `GL`-invariant. `mahlerVolumeAt μ S z` translates `S` by `-z` before taking the polar, and `santaloMahlerVolume μ S` infimizes over `z ∈ interior S`, giving full affine invariance.
- **Banach–Mazur distance:** `banachMazurDist` and `banachMazurEDist` are origin-fixed linear multiplicative distances. `affineBanachMazurDist` and `affineBanachMazurEDist` allow translations (recentering). The `ℝ≥0∞`-valued `*EDist` variants return `⊤` on infeasible pairs, while the real-valued infima inherit Mathlib's `sInf ∅ = 0`.
- **Steiner symmetrization and product norms:** `steinerSymm` replaces each nonempty line fiber by the centered interval of length `sSup - sInf`. On compact convex sets this agrees with measure-theoretic Steiner symmetrization (`bodySteinerSymm` packages this on `ConvexBody (ℝ × E)`), while on disconnected sets it fills gaps between the endpoints. `ℝ × E` carries Mathlib's product max-norm; Euclidean distance bounds (`steinerSymm_dist_le_diam`) are written explicitly. Arbitrary-direction Steiner symmetrization `bodySteinerSymmDir hu K` splits `E ≃ₗ[ℝ] ℝ × orthoHyperplane u`.
- **Minkowski surface area:** `minkowskiSurfaceArea` is the lower outer Minkowski content (`liminf` as `t → 0⁺` of `(volume (K + t • B₁) - volume K) / t`).

## Proof Routes, Scope Limits & Textbook Divergences

Several classical theorems admit multiple proof routes in the literature. Below we record the exact routes chosen here, along with scope limits and divergences from standard textbook presentations (also summarized in [`formalization.yaml`](../formalization.yaml)):

- **Prékopa–Leindler & Brunn–Minkowski (`Volume/BrunnMinkowski.lean`):** Proved via 1D Brunn–Minkowski on compacts → layer-cake + Tonelli induction on `Fin d → ℝ` (`prekopa_leindler_pi`) → orthonormal-basis transport to any finite-dimensional real inner product space `E`, avoiding separate brick/box decomposition machinery. Brunn–Minkowski is derived directly from Prékopa–Leindler. Equality characterization (homothety) is not yet formalized.
- **Rogers–Shephard (`Volume/BrunnConcavity.lean`):** Follows the covariogram route: $g(x) = \mathrm{vol}(K \cap (K + x))$ has $g^{1/d}$ concave (via Brunn concavity of slices), giving $g(x) \ge (1 - \mu_{K - K}(x))^d \mathrm{vol}(K)$ and integrating over $K - K$ in polar coordinates.
- **Euclidean Isoperimetric Inequality (`Volume/Isoperimetric.lean`):** Stated and proved for the lower outer Minkowski content `minkowskiSurfaceArea` via the first variation of Brunn–Minkowski (`minkowski_first_inequality`), rather than geometric measure theory perimeter or Mathlib's `hausdorffMeasure`. Equality on Euclidean balls is verified (`minkowskiSurfaceArea_closedBall`), while the identification of `minkowskiSurfaceArea` with `(d - 1)`-dimensional Hausdorff measure of `frontier K` on full-dimensional convex bodies is not formalized here (note also that for degenerate `(d - 1)`-dimensional convex bodies, which `ConvexBody` includes, lower outer Minkowski content is twice the `(d - 1)`-dimensional Hausdorff measure). Characterization of equality (balls) is not yet formalized.
- **Weyl–Minkowski $V \to H$ Duality (`Polytope/Faces.lean`):** Proved via polarity: for a full-dimensional polytope with `0 ∈ interior (convexHull ℝ S)`, `polarBody S` is compact with finitely many extreme points (`finite_extremePoints_polarBody`), and the Bipolar Theorem yields the irredundant halfspace representation `weyl_minkowski_halfspaces`.
- **Fritz John's Theorem, Löwner's Theorem & Banach–Mazur Compactum (`Asymptotic/JohnEllipsoid.lean`):**
  - Existence of the maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid is proved via compactness of inscribed positive semidefinite linear maps (`PosSemidef`) and spectral perturbation `det(I + εS)`. John's decomposition of the identity is proved via Hahn–Banach separation in the space of symmetric operators on arbitrary `E` (without fixing coordinates `ℝⁿ`), yielding both the general (`d`) and origin-symmetric (`√d`) inclusions, Dvoretzky–Rogers, attainment, and compactness via Blaschke Selection.
  - Both directions of the characterization (`isJohnPosition_iff_john_decomposition`) and uniqueness of the maximal-volume inscribed ellipsoid (`eq_closedBall_of_isJohnPosition_of_det_eq_one`, `unique_max_det_inscribed_ellipsoid`) are proved via spectral AM–GM (`Real.log_le_sub_one_of_pos`). By polarity (`isLoewnerPosition_of_polar_isJohnPosition`, `eq_closedBall_of_isLoewnerPosition_of_det_eq_one`), `loewner_theorem_symmetric` establishes explicit volume minimality (`IsLoewnerPosition`) and uniqueness of the minimal enclosing ellipsoid.
- **Loomis–Whitney, Brascamp–Lieb & Ball's Reverse Isoperimetry (`Asymptotic/BrascampLieb.lean`):**
  - `loomis_whitney` is proved via inductive generalized Hölder.
  - **Scope limits:** Brascamp–Lieb (`brascamp_lieb_rankOne`) and Barthe's reverse form (`reverse_brascamp_lieb_rankOne`) are formalized in the **rank-1 geometric (John decomposition) form** rather than the general higher-rank subspace form. While forward `brascamp_lieb_rankOne` holds for arbitrary measurable `ℝ≥0∞`-valued functions, `reverse_brascamp_lieb_rankOne` currently requires each `f i` to be continuous, strictly positive, and integrable on a nonempty open interval `I i`. Both are proved via Barthe's (1998) 1D monotone Gaussian CDF transport (`exists_gaussian_transport`) + Jacobian change of variables + `IsJohnDecomposition.det_sum_rankOne_ge_prod`, avoiding heat-flow PDE machinery while providing the full engine for Ball's volume-ratio theorems (`ball_volume_ratio`, `ball_volume_ratio_symmetric`), Ball's reverse isoperimetric inequalities (`ball_reverse_isoperimetric`, `ball_reverse_isoperimetric_symmetric`), and Barthe's outer volume-ratio bound (`barthe_outer_volume_ratio_symmetric`).
- **Gaussian & Spherical Concentration, JL & Dvoretzky (`Asymptotic/GaussianConcentration.lean`):**
  - **Mathlib measure:** Uses Mathlib's `ProbabilityTheory.stdGaussian` directly across both the library and `Challenge.lean`, bridged to the explicit Lebesgue density via `stdGaussian_eq_withDensity`.
  - **Proof route:** Gaussian enlargement (`gaussian_enlargement_two_sets`) and Lipschitz MGF / tail bounds (`gaussian_lipschitz_concentration_mean`, `gaussian_lipschitz_concentration_median`) are proved directly from `prekopa_leindler` and Pisier's interpolation / Maurey–Pisier argument rather than Gaussian isoperimetry (Borell–Sudakov–Tsirelson).
  - **Proof route:** Spherical concentration (`spherical_lipschitz_concentration_median`) is deduced from Gaussian concentration via radial polar factorization (`measurePreserving_homeomorphUnitSphereProd_stdGaussian` and `stdGaussian_polar_cone`) rather than Lévy's spherical isoperimetric inequality.
  - **Explicit constants:** Because these routes prioritize short, self-contained proofs without sharp isoperimetric constants, the formalized statements carry explicit, verified (non-optimal) constants: `576` in `spherical_lipschitz_concentration_median`, `16 * log N / ε² ≤ k` in `johnson_lindenstrauss`, and `8192 * k * log(48 / ε) ≤ ε² * log d` in `dvoretzky_theorem` (following Ball's Lecture 9 / Milman's Gaussian proof; note that `k ≥ 1` requires `log d > 8192 * log 48 ≈ 3.2 × 10⁴`).
- **Blaschke–Santaló & Urysohn (`Asymptotic/BlaschkeSantalo.lean`):**
  - **Proof route:** Proved via the **Meyer–Pajor (1990)** slice-volume + Brunn–Minkowski proof that arbitrary-direction Steiner symmetrization does not decrease the Santaló Mahler volume (`santaloMahlerVolume_le_bodySteinerSymmDir`), paired with strict decrease of the second moment $\int_K \|x\|^2\,dx$ (`secondMoment_bodySteinerSymmDir_lt`) and John-position compactness (`𝒞 = {L | B₁ ⊆ L ⊆ d B₁}`) to force any maximizer–moment-minimizer to be a Euclidean ball. Characterization of equality (ellipsoids) is not yet formalized.
  - **Urysohn's inequality:** Derived directly from `blaschke_santalo_real` + `urysohn_of_santalo_at` on bodies with nonempty interior, with empty-interior convex bodies handled directly via `addHaar_frontier`.

## Verification

Verification uses standard Lean, Lake, and Mathlib community tools, run in [`.github/workflows/ci.yml`](../.github/workflows/ci.yml):

| Check | Command | What It Checks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Build** | `lake build ConvexGeom Solution --wfail` | Compiles the full library and Comparator solution with `weak.linter.mathlibStandardSet = true` and warnings treated as errors. |
| **Test suite** | `lake test` | Builds [`ConvexGeomTest`](../ConvexGeomTest.lean), running concrete regression examples and public-import checks across all four layers. |
| **Environment linters** | `lake lint` | Runs Batteries `runLinter` over `ConvexGeom` (`unusedArguments`, `simpNF`, `docBlame`, etc.). |
| **Root import check** | `lake exe mk_all --check --lib ConvexGeom` | Ensures [`ConvexGeom.lean`](../ConvexGeom.lean) imports every module in `ConvexGeom/`. |
| **Axiom audit** | `leanprover-community/axiom-audit` (via `lean-action`) | Verifies every declaration under `ConvexGeom` depends transitively only on `{propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound}` (catches `sorryAx`, `Lean.ofReduceBool`, and custom axioms). |
| **Kernel replay** | `lake env leanchecker <Module>` (looped over leaf modules and `Solution` in CI) | Replays each compiled `.olean` module environment through Lean's bundled `leanchecker` (per module to avoid a Lean `v4.36.0` multi-module `leanchecker` crash). |
| **Metadata validation** | `python3 scripts/validate-formalization.py --schema <v0.4.schema.json>` | Validates [`formalization.yaml`](../formalization.yaml) against the pinned `v0.4` JSON Schema, cross-checks [`comparator.json`](../comparator.json) and [`Challenge.lean`](../Challenge.lean), and validates [`CITATION.cff`](../CITATION.cff) against the pinned CFF 1.2.0 schema. |
| **Sandboxed Comparator** | `bash scripts/verify-comparator.sh [--paranoid] [--check-lib]` | Runs `lake comparator` inside a `bwrap` sandbox comparing Mathlib-only [`Challenge.lean`](../Challenge.lean) against [`Solution.lean`](../Solution.lean) (30 classical theorems), exporting via `leanexport` and replaying through Lean's kernel plus bundled independent checkers (`nanoda_bin` and `con-ron` by default; all 5 bundled checkers with `--paranoid`). As documented by Comparator, the trust base consists of the human-reviewed `Challenge.lean` and its Mathlib imports, the kernels, and the sandbox. |
